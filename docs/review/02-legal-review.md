# Pour-Sona — Legal Review

**Audience:** Legal counsel
**Goal:** Approve consumer-facing copy, identify liability surfaces around AI recommendations for alcohol verticals, and clear the data-processor chain.

---

## 1. What's being sold and to whom

**Customer:** Independent craft-beverage venues (breweries, wineries, distilleries, coffee roasters). Business-to-business SaaS contract.

**End user of the venue's instance:** The venue's *guests* — anonymous, scan a QR code on a table card or menu, receive a product recommendation through a short AI chat.

**Pricing:** $79 / month flat. 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start the trial. Vendor cancels via Stripe customer portal.

**No direct sale to consumers.** The guest experience is bundled into the venue's service; Pour-Sona has no contractual relationship with the consumer.

## 3. Liability surfaces (highest priority for review)

### 3.1 AI-generated alcohol recommendations

The AI recommends specific in-stock products to a guest based on a short conversation about taste preference, mood, and occasion. For alcohol verticals (brewery, winery, distillery), this is the central feature.

**Mitigations already in place:**
- Hard age-gate copy on the welcome and confirmation screens for alcohol verticals (`app/r/[slug]/page.tsx`).
- The model is **only** allowed to recommend products that exist in the venue's live in-stock catalog (`validateRecAgainstCatalog` in `lib/chat-guardrails.ts`). Hallucinated SKUs are dropped.
- No transaction is consummated through Pour-Sona; the guest "orders" via staff, who serve as the responsible-service backstop.
- The recommendation card never offers a quantity recommendation beyond "this pour" or "this flight" — no "have three of these" language.

**Questions for counsel:**
1. Is the existing "Must be 21+. Please enjoy responsibly." disclaimer sufficient, or do we need state-by-state variation (e.g. NY, FL, TX where vendors are located)?
2. Should the **AI nature** of the recommendation be explicitly disclosed to the guest — e.g. "This recommendation was generated by an AI guide trained on {Venue}'s menu"?
3. Does Pour-Sona need to require an age-gate **click-through** (modal blocking access) rather than passive disclaimer copy, for the guest flow on alcohol verticals?
4. Liability between Pour-Sona and the venue when an AI recommends a product to a person who turns out to be underage — does our Terms allocate this risk clearly to the venue, who is the licensed server?

### 3.2 Recommendation accuracy / consumer reliance

The AI describes flavor profiles, body, pairing notes, etc. The model has no out-of-catalog grounding — it works from the venue's own catalog text, which the venue supplies.

**Mitigations:**
- Prompt instructions explicitly tell the model "set null when you have nothing real to say" for optional fields (pairing, upsell, cocktail context). See `lib/agent/build-prompt.ts`.
- The reveal UI shows a "Why this fits you" block tied to the guest's stated preferences, not absolute claims.

**Question:** Should there be a passive "AI recommendations are suggestions, not guarantees" disclaimer somewhere in the guest UX? Right now there isn't one.

### 3.3 Vendor-supplied content

Vendors enter their own brand voice, vocabulary, story, recommendation rules. The AI uses this verbatim (after sentinel sanitisation).

**Mitigation:** `lib/security.ts::sanitizePromptInput()` strips `===WORD===` patterns to prevent vendors from injecting fake recommendation blocks. Otherwise vendor content is trusted.

**Question:** What's our recourse if a vendor enters defamatory, deceptive, or illegal language that the AI then says to a guest? Is the Terms' indemnification language clear enough?

### 3.4 Scraping vendor websites

Vendor signup begins by **fetching the vendor's own website** to extract menu, brand color, contact email, etc.

**Mitigations:**
- `validateScrapeUrl()` is SSRF-hardened — blocks loopback, private IP ranges, cloud-metadata addresses, IPv6 link-local, embedded credentials, and `.local` / `.internal` hostnames.
- 8-second timeout, 6KB content cap.
- User-Agent identifies the bot: `Mozilla/5.0 PoursonaBot/1.0`.
- We only scrape sites the user explicitly enters at signup — never a discovered site.

**Question:** Does the act of fetching a vendor's homepage at vendor-initiated signup require any disclosure / opt-in beyond what's in our Terms?

### 3.5 Prospect Pipeline (internal-only feature)

The Pour-Sona team uses an internal feature at `/poursona-admin/pipeline` to:
1. AI-search the web for independent venues by location + vertical.
2. Fetch each venue's website to score them as sales prospects.
3. Auto-draft a personalized cold-outreach email to the venue.

**This is internal sales tooling — not visible to vendors or guests.**

**Questions:**
1. The outreach emails are sent **from** `andy@pour-sona.com` to discovered email addresses on the venue's site. Is there a CAN-SPAM consideration here even though these are personalized 1-to-1 emails referencing the recipient's specific business?
2. The contact emails are harvested from publicly available HTML on the venue's site (mailto: links or visible address strings). Acceptable scope under U.S. anti-spam / GDPR norms?

### 3.6 Saved leads / CRM (internal-only)

`prospect_leads` and `prospect_activities` tables store: business name, URL, public email, scraped social URLs, status, internal notes, AI-generated draft message, activity log of who-touched-when.

**Question:** Retention policy for unclosed leads — should there be a default purge horizon?

## 4. Data-processor chain

Every third party that touches user data:

| Processor | Data flowing in | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Vercel** | Every request (hosting + DNS) | `vercel.com/andy-wiises-projects/pour-sona` — DPA signed at account level |
| **Neon** | Postgres rows (retailers, products, sessions, orders, leads, etc.) | `console.neon.tech` — DPA needed |
| **Clerk** | Authentication identities (email, optional name, Clerk user id) for vendors and the Pour-Sona team | `dashboard.clerk.com` — DPA / privacy terms |
| **Anthropic** | The AI conversation: guest messages, the vendor's brand context, the venue's catalog | `console.anthropic.com` — Anthropic terms include "we do not train on API content by default" |
| **Stripe** | Vendor billing identity, payment method, subscription state. **No PII flows from Pour-Sona about consumers.** | `dashboard.stripe.com` — DPA built into Stripe terms |
| **Resend** | Vendor email addresses + transactional message bodies (trial expiring, AI cap, order receipt, vendor invite) | `resend.com` — DPA needed |
| **Upstash** | Rate-limit counters keyed by IP. **No PII** beyond the IP itself for the duration of the sliding window. | `console.upstash.com` |
| **Sentry** | Error traces (may contain PII fragments — though we configure scrubbing in `sentry.*.config.ts`) | `sentry.io` — DPA |
| **Google Workspace** | `andy@pour-sona.com` mailbox + future team mailboxes | `admin.google.com` — DPA via Workspace agreement |
| **GitHub** | Source code (private repo) | `github.com/awisecoho/Pour.sona` |

**Data location:** Vercel `iad1`, Neon `iad1` — all U.S. East. No EU users today, no cross-border transfer.

## 5. PII inventory

| Data class | Where stored | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor owner email | `retailers.owner_email` (Neon) | Vendor signup form |
| Vendor team emails | `admin_users.email`, `admin_users.clerk_user_id` (Neon) | Vendor's own team-management page |
| Pour-Sona team emails | `poursona_team.email` (Neon) | Internal onboarding |
| Customer email (optional) | `orders.customer_email`, `sessions.guest_email` (Neon) | Order form — guest optional |
| Customer name (optional) | `orders.customer_name` (Neon) | Order form — guest optional |
| Chat transcripts | `sessions.messages` JSONB (Neon) | The guest's conversation with the AI |
| Stripe customer / subscription IDs | `retailers.stripe_customer_id` (Neon) | Stripe webhook |
| Prospect contact info (internal) | `prospect_leads.email`, `.contact_url`, social URLs (Neon) | Public scrape during sales prospecting |
| IP for rate-limiting | Upstash key, short TTL | Inferred from `x-forwarded-for` header |

**No payment data is stored by Pour-Sona.** Stripe handles card data end-to-end (Stripe Elements / Checkout).

**No tracking cookies, no analytics SDKs.** Funnel analytics live in our own `events` table.

## 7. Open legal questions

1. **AI-disclosure copy.** Should the guest UX explicitly identify the chat partner as an AI? If yes, where: welcome screen, first AI message, footer, or all three?
2. **Age-gate hardening.** Disclaimer-only vs. blocking modal click-through for alcohol verticals.
3. **State-by-state alcohol-recommendation rules.** Any jurisdictions in which the model's recommendation copy needs adjustment (dry counties, ABC commission rules)?
4. **Indemnification flow** vendor ↔ Pour-Sona when an AI recommendation is later disputed by a guest.
5. **CAN-SPAM** posture on the Prospect Pipeline outreach (§3.5).
6. **Lead retention** policy default (§3.6).
7. **Data Processing Addendum** template for vendors who request one.
8. **No EU users today** — but the marketing site is publicly accessible globally. Confirm comfort that a U.S.-only Privacy Policy is acceptable until we deliberately accept EU signups.
